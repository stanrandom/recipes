# easy bolognese

Combine the tomato passata and tinned tomatoes into a pan with green herbs and spices (TBD) and pepper, and simmer.

Cook the pasta as per the packet's instructions, but add oil to the pot for cooking because it makes it nice.

Brown the meat then add it to the pan of tomato sauces.

Drain the pasta (reserve a cup of starchy water), then add butter and minced garlic.

Mix the starchy pasta water in with the red sauces for _reasons_.

Put the pasta in a bowl and top it with the red stuff from the pan.

# Herb-Spiced Turkey Mini Pies

## Servings

4 mini pies (about 2-3 servings depending on size)

## Ingredients

- 250 grams turkey mince (about 0.55 pounds)
- 60 grams all-purpose flour (about 0.5 cup)
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon mixed dried herbs (such as thyme, rosemary, parsley)
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon olive oil
- 1 egg (for egg wash)
- Optional: a pinch of additional herbs or spices for flavor

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 200°C (392°F).
2. In a bowl, combine the turkey mince with salt, dried herbs, pepper, and a pinch of any additional spices you enjoy.
3. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the seasoned turkey mince and cook until browned and cooked through, about 5-7 minutes.
4. While the turkey cooks, prepare your Yorkshire pudding batter: in a separate bowl, mix the flour, a pinch of salt, and enough water (about 150 ml or 2/3 cup) to make a runny batter. Whisk well.
5. Once the turkey is cooked, divide it evenly into small oven-safe ramekins or muffin tin cavities.
6. Pour the Yorkshire pudding batter over the cooked turkey in each container, filling about 2/3 full.
7. Beat the egg and brush some over the top of each pudding for a shiny finish.
8. Bake in the preheated oven for 20-25 minutes until the puddings are risen and golden.
9. Serve warm. Enjoy your herb-spiced turkey mini pies!

# Ranch-Style Dressing

- 1tsp dried oregano 
- 1 garlic clove
- 7.5ml white wine vinegar
- 50ml mayonnaise 

Peel and  grate the garlic. 

Add the mayo to a bowl with
- [ ] the dried oregano
- [ ] grated garlic
- [ ] the white wine vinegar
- [ ] 1/2 tsp cold water
- [ ] a generous pinch of salt and pepper. 

Give everything a good mix up - this is your ranch-style dressing.

# Apple oat muffins

from the internet (@homestead.wellness.daily, maybe)

## ingredients

- 2 apples (approx 200g), grated.
- 2 large eggs.
- rolled oats 160g.
- honey 30g.
- cinnamon (quantity tbd).

## method

1. Grate the apples using a box grater (unpeeled but let's see how that goes).
2. Mix the grated apples, eggs, rolled oats and honey and cinnamon together thoroughly in a bowl until a cohesive muffin (good evening reading) batter forms.
3. Divide the mixture evenly into a 12-cup muffin pan.
4. Bake at fan 160C (maybe less?) for 30 minutes or until golden, set, and baked through.
5. Let cool slightly before serving.

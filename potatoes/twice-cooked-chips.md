# Twice-Cooked Chips for Two People

## Instructions:

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C.
2. Boil a kettle.
3. Chop three potatoes into chips. Add them to a pan of boiling water with a pinch of salt and bring to the boil.
4. Cover the pan, reduce the heat to medium, and cook for 10-12 minutes.
5. Drain the chips and shake them in a colander to rough up the edges.
6. Add the chips to a lined baking tray and cover them with oil, gently salting.
7. Place the tray in the oven and bake for 30 minutes, tossing the chips gently at the 15-minute mark.
8. Serve promptly.

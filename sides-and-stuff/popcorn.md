# Popcorn

Impossible to get wrong.

Get the popcorn maker from the utility room, and the popcorn from the cupboard. Put popcorn into the blue plastic saucepan type thing and then pour that into the maker, and put a very large glass bowl in front of the output spout nozzle (not a metal bowl because it gets hot). Put a tea towel over the exit aperture else the popcorn will fly literally everywhere.

Once it's all popped or nearly all popped or something, put a very big knob of butter into a pan on the hob and melt the knob then put the melted butter onto the popcorn and add some salt (very much) and then shake it all up by shaking it or folding it over or something.

Then probably serve it.
